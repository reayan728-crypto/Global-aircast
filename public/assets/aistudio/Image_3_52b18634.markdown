## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for next-generation environmental science, atmospheric forecasting, and real-time planetary monitoring. The brand personality balances hyper-precise meteorological engineering with calm, humanistic clarity. It avoids both sterile enterprise dashboards and alarmist emergency interfaces, delivering instead an authoritative, reassuring, and atmospheric experience.

The visual style is **Ambient Glassmorphism**. Floating, translucent frosted-glass modules sit directly above reactive sky gradient canvasses that smoothly adapt to prevailing environmental conditions. Crisp ink lines, structural micro-borders, and high-readability grotesque typography anchor dynamic data points, predictive cone envelopes, and radial air chronometers.

## Colors

The color system operates across two interlocked layers: structural typography/chrome and dynamic atmospheric state tokens.

### Chromatic Strategy
- **Primary Ink (`#14202e`)**: High-contrast, dense slate navy. Anchors titles, primary figures, active selections, and focal SVG lines.
- **Secondary Atmospheric Sky (`#bfe3ff`)**: Airy daylight tint representing unpolluted skies, used in brand backdrops, primary accents, and subtle tints.
- **Tertiary Vitality (`#00b050`)**: Clean emerald baseline for safe, healthy environmental conditions.
- **Neutral Muted Slate (`#4d5d70`)**: Calibrated for sub-headers, axis labels, explanatory notes, and micro metadata without losing legibility.

### Dynamic AQI Spectrum
Six regulatory categories define the analytical scale:
- `aqi-good` (`#00b050`): Optimal clean air.
- `aqi-satisfactory` (`#92d050`): Acceptable air quality with minimal impact.
- `aqi-moderate` (`#ffc000`): Threshold value; cautionary for sensitive respiratory groups.
- `aqi-poor` (`#ff7f27`): Unhealthy ambient particulate density.
- `aqi-very-poor` (`#e53935`): Critical warning threshold.
- `aqi-severe` (`#7b1f1f`): Hazardous emergency state.

The application viewport background interpolates across the coordinated `sky-*-start` and `sky-*-end` pairs over a 600ms transition whenever the focal observation location or time step shifts.

## Typography

The type system is built on **Bricolage Grotesque**, providing an editorial, scientific voice with human warmth. Its deep ink traps and confident geometric shapes render dense statistical air metrics approachable without reducing precision.

### Typographic Rules
- **Tabular Numerics**: Enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on all continuous data readouts, real-time scrubbing outputs, confidence boundaries, and coordinate labels to eliminate horizontal reflow during state updates.
- **Optical Sizing**: Enable dynamic optical sizing (`opsz@12..96`) to maintain readability across micro-axis scales (10px) through massive center dial displays (46px).
- **Proportional Tightening**: Apply negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) on headings and numerical displays above 19px to preserve visual density.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a fluid 12-column foundation constrained within a generous max-width of `1240px` with dynamic outer gutter margins. The spacing follows an 8px base rhythm reinforced with 4px/2px subdivisions for tight instrumentation clusters.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Reflow
- **Desktop (above 1100px)**: Asymmetric 2-column operational grid (`minmax(320px, 5fr) 7fr`), positioning real-time circular chronological instruments adjacent to multi-horizon trend charts. Secondary diagnostic cards split 50/50 (`1fr 1fr`).
- **Tablet (901px – 1100px)**: Primary operational grid retains asymmetric ratio; bottom metric insight clusters collapse into single-column stacks (`1fr`).
- **Mobile (<= 900px)**: Fluid single-column reflow (`1fr`). Internal card padding drops to `space-md` (12px), canvas outer padding compresses to 16px, and display headers adopt mobile tokens.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **diffuse atmospheric transparency and low-contrast borders** rather than dark drop shadows. Surfaces appear as suspended physical glass planes over the ambient sky canvas.

### Depth Architecture
- **Base Canvas**: Ambient radial/linear gradient reflecting prevailing air metrics with a `0.6s` cubic-bezier easing interpolation.
- **Glass Planes (Cards & Panels)**: Fills use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.58)` coupled with an active hardware-accelerated `-webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(10px)` and an edge border of `1px solid rgba(20, 32, 46, 0.14)`.
- **Chart & Dial Overlays**: Sub-surfaces use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` for center masks to prioritize legibility, with SVG uncertainty bands mapped at `0.12` opacity.
- **Dynamic Luminance**: Pulse animations illuminate live telemetry dots with radiating aura rings (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 6px rgba(0, 176, 80, 0)`), providing living feedback without distracting from data readouts.

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses a proportional hierarchy scaled to component mass:
- **Base Components (`roundedness: 2`, 8px)**: Standard controls, form inputs, city selection chips, and segmented pills.
- **Micro Badges & Pipeline Tags (6px)**: Compact status flags and categorical tags.
- **Metric Tracks & Bars (4px)**: SHAP influence bars and progress meters.
- **Structural Containers (`rounded-xl`, 22px)**: Primary glass modules, forecast viewports, and narrative summary cards.
- **Pills & Indicator Orbs (9999px / 50%)**: Telemetry status dots and quick-toggle toggles.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Chips
- **Filter Chips**: Rendered with 8px radius, `7px 14px` padding, 13px semi-bold label, and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.58)` glass fill. Border is `1px solid rgba(20, 32, 46, 0.14)`.
- **Active Selection**: Inverts to solid Primary Ink (`#14202e`) background with pure `#ffffff` text and crisp `#14202e` outline.
- **Focus State**: Keyboard navigation displays a vibrant `3px solid #1f6feb` ring with `2px` offset.

### Metric Cards & Structural Panels
- Glass container with `22px` border radius, `18px` inner padding, and `1px solid rgba(20, 32, 46, 0.14)` border over `blur(10px)`.
- Card headers pair an `H2` (`headline-md`, 19px, weight 800) with utility actions or live indicators.

### Status Badges & Pipeline Pills
- **Category Badge**: `6px` radius, `5px 10px` padding, 13px weight 600 text. Dynamically inherits background tint and stroke matching the active AQI category (`COL[c]`).
- **Telemetry Live Pill**: Houses a centered 9px indicator dot pulsing at `1.6s` intervals (`#00b050`) alongside uppercase tracking labels.

### Input Fields & Select Controls
- Styled with `7px 10px` padding, 8px radius, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` background, and subtle slate edge stroke. 
- Scrubbing range sliders use a custom 4px track with Primary Ink draggable thumbs and tabular counter readouts.

### Air Clock & Forecast Visualizations
- **Radial Clock (`#dial`)**: Donut chart with procedural SVG arc wedges. Selected hour wedges feature a `2.5px` ink stroke highlight. Inner hub features a centered 46px tabular readout with secondary PM2.5 unit annotations.
- **Uncertainty Chart (`#chart`)**: Polyline forecast paths (`2.5px` stroke) surrounded by an 80% confidence interval polygon (`0.12` opacity) and segmented category background thresholds (`0.13` opacity).
